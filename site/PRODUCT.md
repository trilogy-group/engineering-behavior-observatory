# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Research partners: evaluation leads and researchers at model labs and harness vendors who receive EBO studies or
evidence packets. They open the page from a link in an email or a packet, at a desk in daylight, with their own
evaluation reports in the next tab. They read skeptically, looking for where each number comes from and whether
they could recompute it. Engineers adopting the CLI are a secondary audience served by the docs.

## Product Purpose

The Engineering Behavior Observatory captures an engineering agent's native trajectory and workspace changes across
seven harnesses, normalizes them into one event schema only after capture qualification, evaluates behavior with
cited LLM judgments that can abstain, and publishes claims whose numbers recompute and whose cited native lines
re-hash. The site exists to show a partner, on the first screen, that EBO's findings are traceable to evidence, and to
route them to the tour, the docs and the quickstart. Success: a partner trusts the method enough to open a packet and
check it.

## Brand Personality

Calibrated, exacting, archival. The voice of an instrument's data release: specific claims, exact units, nothing
inflated. Confidence comes from showing the mechanism (the record, the hash, the recomputation), never from
adjectives. Emotional goal: "I can check this myself."

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: radial gradient glows, grid-mask hero backgrounds, glow shadows, glass blur, identical
  icon-card grids, big-number hero metrics, tiny uppercase eyebrows above every section.
- AI-company marketing gloss and "vibe" copy: superlatives, unverifiable metrics, testimonial strips.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (italic display serif, drop caps) on what is an instrument, not a magazine.
- Terminal cosplay: monospace everywhere as shorthand for "technical".

## Design Principles

1. Show the mechanism. Every claim on the page points at the artifact that backs it: a plate, a record shape, a schema,
   a command.
2. Instrument, not advertisement. Spec sheets, annotated plates and signal paths instead of cards and slogans.
3. Exploratory is labeled exploratory. Illustrations say they are illustrations; viewer numbers are exploratory; only
   EBO's aggregation reports are certified.
4. Data colors mean data. The Atlas categorical and diverging colors appear only where they encode Atlas data.
5. Precision over volume. Exact durations, versions and names; short sentences; no filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1, visible focus, keyboard-reachable controls (including video
chapters and plate annotations), light and dark themes, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored, categorical data colors
always paired with labels or a legend.
